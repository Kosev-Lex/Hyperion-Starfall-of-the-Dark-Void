# Hyperion-Starfall-of-the-Dark-Void
Arcade style space shmup game for PC and android.

Hyperion Starfall, of the Dark Void

The invasion has begun. Survive the onslaught. Reach the Queen.

Hold the line against an alien fleet that becomes faster, stranger,
stronger and more aggressive with every level.

Precision earns devastating weapons. Survival demands movement, nerve and control.

Your bubble shield is also a weapon against encroachers - use it wisely.

Emp: freeze your enemies with this paralysing beam.

The super weapon blasts them into oblivion.

When all else fails, you can evade with the Dash sidestep.

Laser aliens: you'll have to move fast.

Shockwave aliens: they'll stop you in your tracks.

Hyperion Starfall, of the Dark Void.

A fast paced arcade style game.
